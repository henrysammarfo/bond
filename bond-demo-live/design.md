# design.md — BOND live demo cut

## Look
Paper ledger accents over real product footage. Fast. Clean. Not cinematic trailer.

## Palette
- paper `#E8E4DB`
- ink `#141210`
- cyan strike `#076F87`
- allocate `#1F7A4C`
- pending `#8A5A12`

## Type
- Display decisions: Oswald Bold
- Captions / rail: IBM Plex Mono
- Brand once: Montserrat Black “BOND”

## Motion (keep snappy)
1. Kinetic PENDING slam (0.4s) when Pending appears
2. Vertical paper-push between acts (0.3s)
3. Hard punch-cut to tx hash / decision column — no Ken Burns crawl
4. Cyan hairline seam on cuts only

## Audio
Picture leads VO by ~0.3s. Liam calm. Duck any bed hard under speech.

## Forbidden
Dark grain letterbox, blur-push, Inter/Roboto, purple glow, floating badge spam on UI.
